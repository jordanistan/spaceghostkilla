# Delivery and closeout

[Back to launch pack](00_Consulting_Launch.md)

## Report skeleton

1. **Decision summary:** what the evidence supports, highest-priority actions and practical limits.
2. **Scope:** approved boundary, dates, methodology, evidence reviewed and exclusions.
3. **Findings:** one record per issue, using the fields below.
4. **Action plan:** recommended owner, sequence, dependencies and verification for each action.
5. **Limitations:** missing evidence, untested assumptions, unavailable access and areas not reviewed.
6. **Walkthrough / follow-up:** agreed attendees, private channel and included clarification allowance.

| Confidence | [confirmed / inferred / evidence insufficient] |
| Remediation options and tradeoffs | [practical steps] |
| Verification procedure | [safe approved evidence or separately authorized retest] |
| Status | [open / client reports fixed / independently verified / deferred] |

Do not label a client-reported fix as independently verified. Do not confuse a missing screenshot with a confirmed vulnerability. Do not put confidential findings, customer names, internal identifiers or evidence links into public source.

## Pre-delivery review

- Scope and authorization references match the work actually performed.
- Each finding is supported and distinguishes observation from inference.
- Priorities explain likely impact and required conditions; no false certification or guarantees.
- Evidence excerpts are minimal/redacted and have no credentials.
- Recommended fixes identify change risks and a verification step; production implementation is not silently included.
- Report destination, recipients and access controls match the approved private plan.

A draft invoice, email link or client screenshot does not prove payment or settlement. No new engagement is booked automatically at closeout.
