## Minimal initial inquiry draft

Use this as a human-reviewed draft. Do not send it automatically.

> I'd like to discuss a focused security review of [Entra identity / one CI/CD workflow]. The decision we need help with is [plain-language outcome]. We can provide sanitized configuration evidence. Our preferred timing is [approximate period], and the person who can approve the scope is [business role]. Please let us know the next step for agreeing the boundary and quote.

No tenant identifiers, internal hostnames, repository secrets, customer records, credentials or incident evidence belong in the first email. The production inquiry link opens the sender's email app; it does not send a message or confirm delivery. `support@spaceghostkilla.com` is the existing routed address, but inbox receipt must be verified by the owner.

## Discovery checklist

- What decision should the client be able to make after the report?
- Is this one environment/workflow, or several engagements disguised as one?
- Can sanitized evidence answer the question without direct access?
- Who controls each asset and can authorize the proposed activities?
- Is the client asking for certification, an emergency response, exploitation or production changes? Those are outside the proposed first evidence-review offer.
- Is the evidence available, and can both sides meet the proposed timing?

Record only the minimum needed. Do not promise availability, price, coverage or a deadline before the scope/owner gates pass. For a suspected active incident, stop this intake path and have the client use its approved incident process; this pack is not an emergency response service.
