# Consulting launch and inquiry pack

This pack turns a general security inquiry into a bounded, reviewable engagement. It contains **blank templates only**. Copy working versions to an approved private workspace; never commit filled client records, evidence, signatures, identifiers, credentials or private finances to this public repository or a public Obsidian vault.

## Use the pack in order

1. [Inquiry and fit](01_Inquiry_and_Fit.md): understand one problem with a minimal initial message.
2. [Scope and technical authorization](02_Scope_and_Authorization.md): define exactly what can be reviewed, by whom, when, and under what conditions.
3. [Evidence handling](03_Evidence_Handling.md): agree the private transfer, access and deletion plan before receiving files.
4. [Delivery and closeout](04_Delivery_and_Closeout.md): report findings with their limits, deliver privately, and close access.
5. [Owner decisions](05_Owner_Decisions.md): confirm commercial and operating prerequisites before accepting an engagement.

## Proposed first offer

One focused **Entra identity OR CI/CD evidence review**, covering one agreed environment or workflow. The current services design shows **from $800 as draft pricing**, with a final quote by agreement. This pack does not approve a fixed price, hours, delivery date, invoice or purchase.

Start from sanitized client-supplied configuration evidence. Direct system access, testing, implementation or retesting needs its own explicit written scope and permission. A review does not certify compliance, guarantee security or imply accredited FedRAMP assessment status.

## Stage gates

| Stage | Required evidence before advancing | Stop or defer when |
|---|---|---|
| Inquiry | Business problem, system type, authorized contact role | Secrets or confidential findings are sent unsolicited |
| Qualified | Suitable expertise/capacity and one review boundary | Employer conflict, unclear ownership, unreasonable outcome |
| Scoped | Deliverables, exclusions, quote assumptions and dates reviewed | Scope is expanding without an agreed change |
| Authorized | Verified client authority and written approved activity matrix | A target, activity, provider permission or time window is unclear |
| Ready | Agreed private evidence channel, commercial terms and payment arrangement | Evidence controls, terms or owner approvals remain unresolved |
| Delivered | Reviewed private report and walkthrough | Findings overstate evidence or sensitive material is in public links |
| Closed | Receipt/access removal/retention actions recorded | Access remains active or unresolved follow-up is mistaken for completion |

Payment alone is not authorization. An inquiry is not a booking. Stop work when permission is revoked, the window expires, an incident occurs or the scope boundary is reached. Work resumes only after the appropriate written review.
